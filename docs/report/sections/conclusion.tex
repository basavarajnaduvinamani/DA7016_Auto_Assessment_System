\section{Conclusion}
Auto-Assessment connects rubric-based grading to evidence, review, confidence and a readable marked answer sheet. Its main engineering commitment is that the teacher can inspect how a criterion award was reached and which answers require attention. The report-generation path keeps saved measurements separate from prose and marks unfinished empirical work visibly.

The next evidential step is to complete the reference marking, reading checks, robustness analysis and feedback-improvement study under the stated privacy conditions. Agreement, coverage and learning outcomes remain \pending{final empirical conclusions}. Until those measurements exist, the implemented pipeline demonstrates a testable approach to assisted marking, rather than a validated replacement for a teacher.
