HONEST SCOPE

An assistant that knows when to ask you

Agreement is well below human-to-human agreement, so Red-Ink Grader is built for selective auto-marking with teacher review, not as a replacement for the teacher.

  • ✓Every awarded mark cites a rubric criterion and a verified quote from the answer.
  • ✓Low-confidence answers and text that tries to instruct the grader are flagged for you.
  • ✓Your overrides are recorded with a reason; the original AI mark is kept.
  • ✕Evaluated on short answers only; the handwritten set is small and has one grader.
  • ✕The demo sign-in is not real authentication. Do not upload real student data here.
